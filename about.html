About Us

Building with purpose, designing with care.

LAND3 DESIGN is a property development, construction, renovation and interior design business. We work across Block 7, Boatle 1 and Boatle 2 in Gaborone, Botswana.

Who we are

One team from structure to finish.

We bring together construction, renovation, architectural transformation and interior design. That means one business that can take a property from an unfinished structure to a completed, furnished and dressed space.

Our portfolio is organised by location, so each project can be seen in its place. Construction work shows the before and after stages, and our interior work shows curtains, window treatments and finished rooms.

Vision and commitment

What guides our work.

Vision

To transform spaces and create possibilities for the people who live and work in them.

Quality workmanship

Careful execution, clean finishes and attention to detail on every stage of a build.

Thoughtful design

Practical layouts, considered materials and interiors that suit how each space is used.
