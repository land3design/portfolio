About the business

Architecture, construction and interiors under one roof.

LAND3 DESIGN is a property development, construction, renovation and interior design business. We take properties from raw structure to finished living space, combining practical building skill with considered design.

Our work covers construction and building works, renovations and property improvements, architectural transformation, curtains and window treatments, and interior design and styling.

Our vision

Turn ordinary properties into spaces people are proud to live and work in.

Commitment to quality

Careful workmanship, clean finishes and attention to detail on every project.
